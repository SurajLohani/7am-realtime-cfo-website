Periodic MIS & Reporting

Reporting on the cadence your business actually needs — timed so the numbers reach you in time to decide, not just to know.

The Pain Behind This

“By the time I see the report, the problem is already old.”

If left as-is: Delayed intervention, missed opportunities, and management running the business on stale information.

What you actually need: Early visibility — problems surface before month-end, not after it.

What It Solves

The problem isn't reporting frequency — it's reporting timing

SCATTERED DATA

Data scattered across ERPs, custom software, PDFs, WhatsApp, Excel, emails, drives, departments, branches, entities, currencies and countries.

LATE DECISIONS

Reports arriving after the decision window has already closed.

NO SINGLE TRUTH

No single version of truth across systems.

What We Build

The full architecture behind the numbers you see

This absorbs everything the old “Data Consolidation” step used to cover — it's not a separate product, it's simply how this service is built.

  • Single automated data hub pulling from every source system
  • A reporting cadence set to match how the business actually makes decisions — daily where daily matters (cash, inventory), weekly or monthly where that's the right rhythm
  • Delivered on your preferred channel (WhatsApp, Email, Slack, Telegram, MS Teams, Google Chat)
  • System-to-physical reconciliation so the numbers can be trusted
Who It's For

Built for founders who need reporting timed to decisions

BUSINESS PROFILE

Inventory businesses ₹5Cr+, multi-location or multi-entity.

THE NEED

Founders who need reporting timed to decisions, without hiring a full finance team.

How It Runs

Consolidated, verified, analyzed — delivered as one message

On the agreed cadence, on your preferred channel. No dashboard login required. Need it off-cycle — request it on demand.

Not Either/Or

Periodic MIS & Reporting vs. Fractional CFO — different jobs, same system

Periodic MIS & Reporting — The Flagship

Timed clarity, most businesses run on this.

  • Runs on the cadence your business needs — scheduled automatically, or on demand
  • Cash, variance, inventory and working capital — the numbers, verified
  • Delivered on WhatsApp, Email, Slack, Telegram or Teams — no dashboard login
  • Monthly retainer, scales with your business from day one
Fractional CFO — The Optional Layer

Senior judgment, added when the stakes are higher.

  • Engaged as needed — fundraising rounds, board meetings, lender talks
  • Interprets what the numbers mean for a specific high-stakes decision
  • A person in the room, not a message on a channel
  • Built on top of that visibility — not a substitute for it
