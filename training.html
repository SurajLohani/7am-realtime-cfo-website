The Pain Behind This

“I know the tool. I still can’t tell what the numbers are saying.”

If left as-is: Courses that teach buttons and formulas, then leave you stuck the first time a real finance problem looks different from the example.

What you actually need: Someone who has run real finance functions to teach the thinking behind the tool: what to check, what to question, and what the decision is.

Who It’s For

The same teacher, pitched at your level

Every learner starts from a different place and needs a different path. Tell me where you are and where you want to be.

Students

Start your finance career with a practical foundation

Excel for accounting and finance, how real books and MIS look, and how to build a portfolio before your first job. The book and free resources are a good starting point.

Junior Professionals

Stop copying last month’s file. Learn why the model works, how to check it, and how to make routine reports faster and safer.

Mid-Level & Experienced Professionals

CFOs & Finance Leaders

How to automate without breaking controls, what to hand to AI and what must stay with a human, and how to build a team that can run it.

Founders, Owners & Directors

Understand your own numbers

Read your MIS, cash flow and margins with confidence, and ask your finance team the right questions.

Teams & Institutions

Workshops built around your data and your problems

Hands-on sessions for finance teams, colleges and business schools, online or in person.

What I Teach

Five areas, one connected skill set

  • FP&A — budgeting, rolling forecasts, variance analysis, scenario and sensitivity planning
  • Excel for Finance & Accounting — formulas, Power Query, VBA and model design for real finance work
  • Power BI for Finance — dashboards and management reporting that answer decisions, not just show charts
  • Financial Modelling & MIS — building models and reports that reconcile and can be explained
  • AI & Automation for Finance — n8n, Zapier, Power Automate and AI tools, with the controls that keep them safe

Philosophy

Don’t just teach the tool. Teach the thinking behind the tool.

A tool changes every few years. The habit of asking where a number came from, what could be hiding behind it and what decision it should drive does not.
