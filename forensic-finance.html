Independent Challenge & Control

Forensic Finance & Controls

When the reports look fine but the cash does not, an independent review finds what routine reporting never questions: leakage, mismatches, timing games and control gaps.

Finance Blind Spots addressed: Control blind spots: leakage, mismatches and reconciliation gaps. What are Finance Blind Spots? →

The Pain Behind This

“The numbers reconcile on paper. Why does it feel like money is leaking?”

If left as-is: Leakage, duplicate or inflated payments, misdated expenses and weak controls keep running quietly until a lender, auditor or bad quarter exposes them.

What you actually need: An independent, evidence-based review that shows exactly where the money is going, what it cost, and how to stop it recurring.

What It Covers

Six places leakage usually hides

Reconciliation

Books vs bank, ledger vs subledger

Gaps between what the system says and what actually moved, traced to the entry.

Payments & Vendors

Duplicates, price drift, unfavourable terms

Payment patterns and vendor pricing compared against benchmarks and contracts.

Expense Timing & Cut-off

Costs that shift across periods

Items booked in the wrong month or quarter that make results look better or worse than they are.

Variance Analysis

What moved, how much and why

Budget-to-actual and period-on-period movements broken down to their real drivers.

Inventory & Margin

Where stock and profit quietly disappear

Ageing, shrinkage signals and product- or branch-level margins hidden under group totals.

Controls & Approvals

Who can do what, and who checks

Approval flow, segregation of duties and exception reporting gaps.

How It Works

Evidence first, opinions later

1. Scope

Agree the question

What you suspect, which period, which entities and which data sources.

2. Reconcile

Build one reliable data set

Pull ledgers, bank statements, payables and inventory into one reconciled base before any conclusion.

3. Challenge

Test it independently

Run targeted tests for duplicates, outliers, timing, pricing and approvals, and trace each finding to source.

4. Report & Fix

Findings with evidence and actions

A clear report with amounts, supporting entries and recommended corrections, plus controls and automated alerts so it does not return.

Who It’s For

Four situations where a second pair of eyes pays off

  • Owners and directors — who sense leakage but cannot see where
  • CFOs and finance heads — who want an independent check on their own numbers
  • Businesses preparing to raise funds or borrow — who need clean, defensible numbers before due diligence
  • Boards and lenders — who need an independent view of reporting quality
A Clear Boundary

Finance review, not a legal investigation

This is an independent finance and controls review. Where a matter may lead to legal proceedings, it needs to be handled with qualified legal and audit professionals, and I work alongside them.
