Fractional CFO — also called Virtual CFO, Part-Time CFO, Interim CFO or Outsourced Finance Consultant

Not a tool. Not a report. A CFO. Senior finance leadership without a full-time hire — for board meetings, lender conversations and fundraising rounds where you need a credible finance voice in the room.

The Pain Behind This

“We are making important growth or capital decisions without seeing the full financial impact.”

If left as-is: Poor capital allocation, weak investor readiness, under- or over-investment, and uncertainty going into any transaction.

What you actually need: Capital & strategic decision support — a senior finance voice in the room before the commitment is made, not after.

What It Solves

Decisions that need a senior finance mind in the room — not just the numbers

The moments a growing business needs someone who can represent finance credibly, not just report on it.

THE PROBLEM

Board meetings, lender conversations and fundraising rounds with no one to represent the finance function credibly.

THE GAP

A growing business that has outgrown its accountant but can't yet justify a full-time CFO salary.

THE RESULT

Decisions get made without the senior finance judgment that should be shaping them.

What We Bring

Strategic input, layered on top of your existing visibility

  • Strategic input on fundraising, board reporting and lender relationships
  • Business-OS reviews (the 5-Layer, 17-Stage framework) layered on top of existing Periodic MIS & Reporting visibility
  • Investor-ready narrative, not just investor-ready numbers
  • For businesses that want everything under one senior engagement: Fractional CFO now also covers the full-architecture scope the old “Full Package” used to describe — a wider engagement, not a separate product
Who It's For

An addition to daily clarity, not a replacement for it

  • Businesses already running on Periodic MIS & Reporting who now need a senior finance voice for high-stakes conversations
  • Not a replacement for periodic clarity — an addition to it
