Beyond Finance

Business Automation

Beyond finance — SOPs, workflow automation and process design across other functions of the business.

The Pain Behind This

“The business has grown, but finance still works the way it did when we were smaller.”

If left as-is: Control gaps, process bottlenecks, and management dependency that widen every time the business adds a new entity, department or location.

What you actually need: Scalable control — processes and controls that hold as the business grows, not the ones it started with.

What's Included

The same automation discipline, applied beyond finance

SOPs, workflow automation and process design across other functions of the business — operations, sales, HR — wherever a manual process is costing time it shouldn't.

SOP Design

Standard Operating Procedures, documented and enforced

Processes written down once, properly, so they don't live only in one person's head — and actually get followed.

Workflow Automation

Manual handoffs replaced with automated triggers

Approvals, notifications and task routing that happen automatically instead of over email and WhatsApp follow-ups.

HR Process Automation

Onboarding, attendance and workforce processes systemized

From new-hire onboarding checklists to attendance and leave tracking — taken off spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.

CRM & Sales Process Automation

Lead-to-customer workflow, tracked and automated

Lead capture, follow-up reminders and pipeline tracking systemized so deals don't fall through the cracks.

Procurement & Vendor Workflow

Purchase requests and vendor coordination, automated

Requisitions, approvals and vendor follow-ups moved out of scattered emails into one tracked workflow.

Process Design & Documentation

Process maps built before automation is layered on

The process is mapped and fixed first — automation only gets layered onto a process that actually makes sense.
