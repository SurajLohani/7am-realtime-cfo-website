Built For Where You Are

CFO services for startups. CFO services for established companies.

Same finance discipline, applied differently depending on whether you're building the finance function from zero, or fixing one that's already carrying real weight.

CFO Services for Startups

Build the finance function before it becomes a fire.

Early-stage and growth-stage businesses rarely have a finance function yet — just an accountant closing books, and a founder guessing the rest. This is about putting the basic architecture in place before it's forced on you by a bad month.

  • Basic MIS and cash visibility from day one, not after a crisis
  • Investor-ready numbers before you're asked for them
  • Right-sized controls — enough structure, without slowing you down
CFO Services for Established Companies

Fix what scale already broke.

Established, multi-location or multi-entity businesses usually have a finance function — just one that's slower, more manual and less trustworthy than the business has outgrown. This is about finding where it's leaking and re-architecting it without disrupting operations.

  • Diagnostic first — find where the visibility gap actually is
  • Automation layered onto existing ERP, not a rip-and-replace
  • Fraud and leakage review using the Last Layer Approach™

Two Layers, One System

The Strategy layer finds the money. The Last Layer™ finds the leak.

Most finance audits start and stop at the top — P&L, trial balance, system records. Strategy work (Financial Diagnostic & Control Architecture) fixes what the numbers reveal. The Last Layer Approach™ goes below that, to the warehouse floor, the security guard, the delivery rider, the office boy who handled the physical document. Fraud does not live in boardrooms. It lives in the gap between what the system records and what the last layer knows.

Strategy Layer

Last Layer™
