Business Intelligence

Businesses run on data. Most teams spend more time managing it than understanding it. Excel automation, Power BI dashboards, and AI workflow automation — all in one connected service.

What's Included

One service, three layers of the same problem

  • Excel automation — VBA, Power Query, Power Pivot — replacing manual, error-prone spreadsheet work
  • Power BI, Google Sheets and Tableau dashboards — custom dashboards, MIS and reporting systems, built and automated end-to-end (20+ projects completed)
  • AI workflow automation — structured automation layered on top, so the team spends time on decisions, not re-keying numbers
  • Data Warehouse & ETL Setup — a single connected data layer pulling from every source system, so dashboards aren't rebuilt from scratch every time a source changes
  • Automated KPI Alerts — threshold-based alerts on the metrics that matter, sent the moment a number crosses the line, not discovered at month-end
