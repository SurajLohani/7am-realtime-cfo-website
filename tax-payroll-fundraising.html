Bookkeeping, Tax, Payroll & Fundraising Support, Across Borders

The finance backbone behind a growing business: books that stay current, compliance done on time, payroll that is controlled, investor-ready numbers, and support that works wherever your business operates.

Strong India tax knowledge; for other countries, coordination with local specialists. Fundraising support means investor-ready financial information and due-diligence preparation, not investor sourcing, investment banking or deal advisory.

The Pain Behind This

“We are growing, but compliance, payroll and the investor numbers are all held together by separate people and spreadsheets.”

If left as-is: Missed filings, payroll errors, and a funding conversation that stalls because the numbers do not agree.

What you actually need: One finance lead who owns the backbone, links it to your MIS and cash, and brings in licensed local specialists wherever the law requires.

What It Covers

Six support areas, one connected backbone

Bookkeeping

Books that stay current and reconcile

Day-to-day bookkeeping on Tally, Zoho, Xero, QuickBooks or Excel, with bank and ledger reconciliation, and finalisation of accounts and the balance sheet, so tax, payroll and MIS all start from numbers that agree.

Tax Support

Direct and indirect tax, planned not rushed

Complete command of Indian tax: GST, TDS, ESI, PF, income tax, direct and indirect tax, with returns filed on time. I am an enrolled GST Practitioner (GSTP) under the CGST Act, 2017. For any other country, I study that jurisdiction’s framework as part of scoping your requirement, and work with a licensed local professional wherever the law requires one to sign.

Payroll & Statutory

Payroll as part of the finance system

Structured payroll processing with PF, ESI, gratuity and TDS on salary handled in line, and reconciled to your books.

Fundraising Support

Investor-ready numbers

Financial models, projections, MIS packs and data-room support that agree with each other and can be defended in due diligence. The focus is on the numbers; investor sourcing and term-sheet negotiation are outside this service.

Treasury & Banking

Cash, banks and credit lines in order

Cash and working-capital planning, coordination with banks including consortium meetings, overdraft (OD) and debt facilities, and bank stock audits, with the data and papers bankers ask for prepared in advance.

Import, Export & Forex

Trade finance and currency exposure

Letter of Credit applications, Bill of Entry, 15CA/15CB and customs payment documentation, plus exposure data and analysis for foreign-exchange hedging. Hedging decisions stay with the owner and the bank.

Working Globally

Remote by design

A finance function does not need a postcode.

  • Multi-currency and multi-entity reporting — consolidated views across entities and currencies
  • Your systems — works with Zoho, Tally, other ERPs and plain Excel; no forced migration
  • Time zones — structured reporting cadence and written updates that work across time zones
  • Local compliance — scoped per country; licensed local professionals are brought in where the law requires
  • Same method everywhere — data first, controls second, AI third, with a human responsible at every step
Backbone First

Tell me which part of the backbone is slipping

Books, tax, payroll, funding numbers or all of them: share the situation and the country, and I will scope the right starting point.
