Why This Exists

Cash and profit tell two different stories. Most founders only see one.

The P&L says the business made money. The bank balance says otherwise. Somewhere between the two sits blocked inventory, stretched receivables, and a variance nobody flagged in time — and by the time it shows up in a monthly review, the decision that could have fixed it is already behind you.

This practice exists to close that gap — turning scattered Zoho, Tally, ERP and Excel data into one page that shows where cash actually stands, where it's diverging from profit, and what today's decision should be.

Data First, Rakshak Second, AI Third, Human Responsible always.

Every MIS one-pager answers six questions

No dashboard to open, no report to download — just the six things that actually decide your day, in the order they matter.

  • Cash Position — where you stand right now, today.
  • Cash vs Profit — the gap nobody explains until month-end.
  • Variance Flags — what moved, by how much, and why.
  • Inventory — ageing, slow-moving, blocked capital.
  • Working Capital — DSO, DIO, DPO, cash conversion cycle.
  • Today's Decisions — ranked by urgency, not by inbox order.
